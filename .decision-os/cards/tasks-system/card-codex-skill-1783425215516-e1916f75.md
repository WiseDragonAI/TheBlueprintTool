## Opening Snapshot

- **`decision-os` is a local decision workspace:** a browser canvas, TypeScript ledger server, and workspace-local `.decision-os` data store. Source: `A.1`, `B.1`.
- **The core work happens on ledger canvases:** operators inspect, edit, organize, discuss, and review cards across `specs`, `data`, `performances`, and `tasks-system`. Source: `A.1`, `A.3`, `C.6`.
- **Cards are the main decision objects:** they can hold Markdown, fields, images, files, HTML assets, summaries, reports, and right-side threads. Source: `A.2`, `C.2`, `C.5`.
- **Threads are the main collaboration handoff:** operators write or record notes, agents reply in `# AGENT` sections, and the filesystem remains durable state. Source: `A.4`, `C.5`, `B.5`.

## What Is This Product For?

- **It turns decisions into navigable workspace objects:** ledgers, cards, zones, groups, relationships, and threads make decision work visible. Source: `A.1`, `A.2`, `C.6`.
- **It keeps work local and durable:** the workspace uses `.decision-os` files as the canonical state store. Source: `B.4`, `B.5`.
- **It supports operator-agent collaboration:** operators leave notes and agents respond through durable thread sections. Source: `A.4`, `C.5`, `C.8`.
- **It is currently dogfooded internally:** the active workspace contains active spec, data, performance, and task-system ledgers. Source: `A.1`, `A.5`.

## What Can The Operator Do?

- **Review and edit ledger canvases:** open a ledger route, inspect cards, move objects, and update visible organization. Source: `A.3`, `C.1`.
- **Work with structured canvas objects:** create or manipulate cards, zones, groups, relationships, and selected object sets. Source: `A.2`, `C.2`.
- **Use rich card content:** review Markdown, fields, image carousels, HTML card assets, report cards, and mockups. Source: `A.2`, `C.2`, `C.5`.
- **Capture collaboration context:** write notes, paste files or images, record voice notes, and trigger transcription retry when needed. Source: `A.3`, `B.3`, `C.3`.

## How Is The Workspace Shaped?

- **Ledger tabs separate work modes:** `specs` holds behavior contracts, `data` holds base classes, `performances` holds evidence, and `tasks-system` holds next features. Source: `C.6`.
- **The canvas is the main spatial model:** cards, zones, groups, labels, relationship overlays, and detail layers live on the full-canvas surface. Source: `A.2`, `C.2`.
- **The right side is for threads:** the thread inspector and terminal-style composer carry operator-agent conversation and work status. Source: `C.2`, `A.7`.
- **The ledgers overview is separate:** `/ledgers` exposes a hidden overview surface distinct from individual ledger routes. Source: `C.2`, `C.4`.

## What Controls Matter?

- **`A` opens or focuses the thread panel:** use it when conversation or agent handoff is the next action. Source: `C.7`.
- **`X` controls active voice capture:** start or stop a voice note from the current workflow. Source: `C.7`.
- **`Esc` cancels transient tooling:** cancel voice capture, close thread tooling, or clear selection. Source: `C.7`.
- **`Del`, `Ctrl+C`, `Ctrl+V`, and `Ctrl+D` manage selected objects:** confirm deletion, copy, paste, or resize selected cards and zones. Source: `C.7`.

## How Do Notes, Media, And Reports Fit?

- **Threads are both conversation and work queue:** they currently carry history, queue state, locks, status, progress, and recovery. Source: `A.7`.
- **Voice notes flow through capture, upload, transcription, optimistic note update, and retry.** Source: `C.5`.
- **Card media is persistent:** image dimensions survive canvas zoom through card-level `imageSizes`. Source: `B.4`, `C.9`.
- **HTML reports are card assets:** embedded HTML must stay under allowed active-card asset paths. Source: `A.2`, `C.9`.

## What Decisions Remain Open?

- **Report UX needs a product boundary:** report work could become a card view, ledger type, goal cockpit tab, or embedded reader. Source: `A.7`, `D.5`.
- **Task queues and watcher loops remain operator-owned:** future ownership is not assigned unless a later card makes it explicit. Source: `A.6`, `D.5`.
- **Goal-object UX is still part of the next-feature surface:** `tasks-system` contains goal-object work but not a settled final model. Source: `A.6`, `D.5`.
- **External adoption is unproven in this source:** evidence is internal, with no production analytics or customer support sample found. Source: `A.5`, `D.4`.

## Use Constraints

- **Keep frame production responsive:** pan, zoom, and drag are sensitive to layout writes, zone label reads, overlays, rich cards, and relationship routing. Source: `A.7`, `B.8`, `C.9`.
- **Hydrate expensive detail only when useful:** detailed card surfaces should appear when detail is visible. Source: `C.9`.
- **Do not let thread or voice tooling steal editing keystrokes:** keyboard behavior must preserve canvas and editing flows. Source: `C.9`.
- **Keep local secrets local:** transcription keys and uploaded voice files should stay local and uncommitted. Source: `B.6`, `B.7`.
