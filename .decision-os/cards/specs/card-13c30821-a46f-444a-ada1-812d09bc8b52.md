## A. Symlink-aware discovery

1. Directory discovery now includes symbolic links whose resolved targets exist and are directories.
2. The returned path and display path preserve the lexical alias below the catalog root; filesystem metadata is read from the resolved target.
3. Nested directories remain addressable through the alias, including targets outside the catalog root.
4. Broken links, links to files, absolute paths, lexical traversal outside the catalog, hidden directories, and recursive ancestor cycles remain excluded.
5. Symbolic-link rows expose explicit metadata to the frontend.
