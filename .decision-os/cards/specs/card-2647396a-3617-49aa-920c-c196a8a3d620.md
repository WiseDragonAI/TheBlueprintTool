## A. Implementation Result

1. **Implemented:** ledger cards persist `executionStatus: pending|running` plus `executionRunId` ownership for direct runs, continuations, and pipelines.
2. **Terminal safety:** completion, failure, cancellation, adopted-process settlement, and restart recovery clear execution only when the settling run still owns it.
3. **Lifecycle isolation:** card `status` remains `todo`, `backlog`, or `done`.
