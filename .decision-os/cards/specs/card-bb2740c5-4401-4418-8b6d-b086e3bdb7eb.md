## A. Required decision

1. Define the persisted schedule fields: `id`, `pipelineId`, `ledgerId`, `sourceCardId`, `cron`, `timeZone`, `enabled`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt`, and `nextRunAt`.
2. Define the persisted occurrence fields: `scheduleId`, `scheduledFor`, `enqueuedAt`, `pipelineRunId`, and terminal launch error.
3. Pin cron parsing and IANA time-zone behavior to one maintained backend dependency after verifying its license, release status, and compatibility with the current Node runtime.

---

## B. Acceptance evidence

1. A written schema and validation table covers invalid cron expressions, invalid time zones, deleted pipelines, disabled schedules, daylight-saving transitions, missed occurrences, and clock rollback.
2. The decision states that one missed occurrence is recovered after downtime and older missed occurrences are recorded as skipped.
3. The occurrence identity `(scheduleId, scheduledFor)` is the single idempotency boundary.
