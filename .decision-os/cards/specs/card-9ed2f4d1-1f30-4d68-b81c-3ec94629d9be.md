## A. Scope

1. **Backend:** Add the authoritative `complete-master-task` mutation with complete canonical-link validation.
2. **Mobile UI:** Render the aggregate completion action at the bottom of the master-task detail.
3. **Persistence:** Synchronize every linked card, the master card, and the master-task lifecycle Markdown.

---

## B. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Linked completion:** Every valid linked subtask changes from `todo` to `done`.
2. **Master completion:** The master card changes to `done`, its lifecycle label changes to `#task-complete`, and `Completed at` is recorded.
3. **Invalid request:** An unresolved canonical link leaves every card unchanged.
4. **Tests:** Focused frontend and backend checks pass.
