## A. Scope

1. **Control Room tabs:** Replace the static Queue, Active, and Done subtitles in the mobile Control Room with dynamic task totals.

---

## B. Contract

1. **Count source:** Derive each total from the corresponding `queue`, `active`, and `done` task collection.
2. **Ledger filtering:** Recalculate every total against the selected ledger filter.
3. **Copy:** Render `1 task` for a single task and `<count> tasks` for every other total.

---

## C. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Dynamic totals:** Queue, Active, and Done each display their current task count.
2. **Verification:** The focused mobile Control Room test suite passes.
