## A. Scope

1. **Target:** Verify the implemented fullscreen viewer on `http://127.0.0.1:50150/specs` without restarting the operator-owned server.
2. **Gesture sequence:** On a representative touch viewport, tap the current carousel image, open the viewer, pan, pinch toward both scale limits, close with the fixed button, and inspect the carousel slide after close.

---

## B. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Route evidence:** Record the route and HTTP result proving the operator-facing process serves the changed files and pinned asset.
2. **Behavioral evidence:** Record observations for fade-in reveal, exact active image, pan, pinch zoom, fixed close availability, canvas isolation, and unchanged carousel slide.
3. **Claim calibration:** Keep the master task active and request one focused operator validation when device-level touch verification cannot be performed from the available environment.

---

## C. Evidence

1. **Route:** The existing process serves `http://127.0.0.1:50150/specs` with HTTP `200`; it was not restarted.
2. **Assets:** `http://127.0.0.1:50150/assets/vendor/panzoom-4.6.2.es.js` returns HTTP `200` with `26,822` bytes, and the served viewer module returns HTTP `200` with `minScale: 0.3` and `maxScale: 50`.
3. **Surface:** The running mobile CSS includes the hidden fullscreen trigger, visible fade-in state, fullscreen stage, and fixed safe-area close control.
4. **Blocker:** No browser automation package or device-input bridge is installed in the environment, so the representative tap, pan, and pinch sequence has not been exercised on the target surface.
5. **Operator check:** On one carousel at `/specs`, tap the active image, tap the revealed top-right fullscreen button, pinch and pan the modal image, then close it and confirm the same carousel slide remains selected.

---

## D. Contradicted Device Result

1. **Observed failure:** The operator reports that the fullscreen modal opens on mobile but pinch zoom and pan do not respond.
2. **Completion gate:** Keep both subtasks and the master task active until the first incorrect pointer-to-transform transition is identified, fixed, and exercised on the reported target device.
3. **Required capture:** Obtain the device model, operating-system version, browser and version, exact route, and a short recording of one failed pinch-and-pan attempt; then inspect `pointerdown`, `pointermove`, `pointerup`, `panzoomstart`, `panzoomchange`, and the image's inline `transform` during that attempt.

---

## E. Chromium Verification

1. **Target:** Headless Chromium `138.0.7204.168`, Android mobile viewport `390 × 844`, and the live card route under `http://127.0.0.1:50150/specs`.
2. **Pinch:** Real CDP touch input changed the image from `scale(1) translate(0px, 0px)` to `scale(1.6) translate(-8.05644px, -89.6677px)` and emitted successive `panzoomzoom` and `panzoomchange` events.
3. **Pan:** A one-finger CDP gesture changed translation to `translate(29.4436px, -58.4177px)` and emitted `panzoompan` and `panzoomchange`.
4. **Close and persistence:** The close control remained visible with `position: fixed`; closing the viewer preserved carousel slide index `0` before and after close.
5. **Runtime health:** The trace recorded no console errors and no failed requests.
