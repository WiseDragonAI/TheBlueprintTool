## A. Scope

1. **Arrival event:** Use Embla's `settle` event to reveal the selected slide title after carousel motion finishes.
2. **Visibility timing:** Keep the title visible for `1,000ms` and fade opacity to `0` over `260ms`.
3. **Gesture state:** Hide titles immediately on `pointerDown` and selection change so stale titles do not remain visible during motion.
4. **Cleanup:** Cancel the pending title timer before destroying the Embla instance.

---

## B. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Initial slide:** The initial title appears, remains visible for `1,000ms`, then fades out.
2. **Settled slide:** Every newly settled slide repeats the same title timing.
3. **Unobstructed image:** No title remains visible after the `260ms` fade completes.
4. **Device verification:** Mobile Brave confirms the complete arrival, hold, and fade sequence on the served carousel route.
