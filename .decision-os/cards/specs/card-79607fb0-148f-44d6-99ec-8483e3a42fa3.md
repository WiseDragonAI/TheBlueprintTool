## A. Scope

1. **Task:** Research prominent Codex plugins and explain their concrete use cases using official marketplace, GitHub, and community evidence.

---

## B. Evidence

1. **Official catalog:** Review OpenAI's plugin repository and published role-specific plugin examples.
2. **Independent catalog:** Review the public Codex Plugin Marketplace while identifying it as unaffiliated with OpenAI.
3. **Community:** Review Reddit discussion for recurring recommendations and disagreements.

---

## C. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Examples:** Explain concrete workflows for engineering, design, deployment, knowledge work, and role-specific plugins.
2. **Calibration:** Do not present editorial selections, stars, votes, and anecdotes as authoritative install rankings.
3. **Recommendation:** Give the operator a small, use-case-driven starting set.
