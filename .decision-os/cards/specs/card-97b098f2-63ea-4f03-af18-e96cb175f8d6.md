## A. Unified Card-Thread Lifecycle

1. **Contradicted-success RCA:** The Back and voice paths had local thread cleanup, but the Backlog, completion, deletion, card-to-card, history, and error-view paths still navigated independently. `Move to backlog` therefore hid the card without clearing `card-thread-open`.
2. **Single open boundary:** `renderCard()` now delegates to `openCardDetail()`. Every responsive desktop card opens its owned thread; mobile card detail remains closed. New task intake uses the same route-driven boundary.
3. **Single close boundary:** `navigate()`, final non-card views, and `popstate` delegate to `closeCardDetail()`. Back, Backlog, completion, deletion, `Shift+X`, card-to-card navigation, history, and error exits no longer own separate thread cleanup.
