## A. Corrected Implementation

1. Direct Codex tasks retain the card’s persisted logical `Active since` launch timestamp.
2. Pipeline tasks use the durable pipeline attempt `resumedAt`, falling back to pipeline `createdAt`.
3. Process-segment `startedAt` no longer controls Control Room timers.

---

## B. Regression

1. A direct task remains anchored to its logical launch when process recovery creates a newer segment timestamp.
2. A pipeline task overrides an older card activation with its current durable pipeline attempt timestamp.
3. Commit `2549f01` contains the correction and regressions.
