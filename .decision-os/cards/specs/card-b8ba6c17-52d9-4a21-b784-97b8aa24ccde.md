## A. Deliverable

1. **Setup:** Pair one Termux phone and one workstation with the same private project clone.
2. **Handoff:** Publish workstation state, continue on phone, publish phone state, and reload both.
3. **Remote run:** Launch from phone on workstation and observe acknowledgement, progress, checkpoint, and terminal status.
4. **Reconcile:** Fast-forward the result on phone and confirm it survives reload.
5. **Failures:** Exercise offline queue, dirty refusal, rejected publication, revocation, and lease exclusion.

---

## B. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Target evidence:** Record routes, coordinator URL, HTTP results, devices, systems, Chromium, and Decision OS commit.
2. **Behavior evidence:** Record acquire, optimistic change, persisted reload, and rejected-request reconciliation.
3. **Git evidence:** Record starting SHAs, checkpoint commits, pushed SHAs, and absence of force updates.
4. **Audit evidence:** Record node, lease, command, run, and terminal audit IDs.
5. **Gate:** Keep ledger status `todo` until the served phone interaction and cross-device persistence are observed.
