#master-task

Ledger: Specs

## A. Scope

1. **Control Room:** Show dynamic task totals in the mobile Control Room bottom tabs for Queue, Active, and Done.
2. **Terminology:** Display totals as tasks, with singular and plural copy.

---

## B. Requirements

1. **Live data:** Derive each tab total from the current Control Room task collections.
2. **Filtering:** Keep totals synchronized with the selected ledger filter.
3. **Verification:** Cover the tab totals in the focused mobile Control Room tests.
