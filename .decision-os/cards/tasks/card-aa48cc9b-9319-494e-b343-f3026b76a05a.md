#master-task

Ledger: Specs
Queue rank: 7

## A. Scope

1. **Carousel affordance:** Tapping the active image in a multi-image card carousel must fade in a compact fullscreen button at the image's top-right corner.
2. **Fullscreen viewer:** Activating that button must open the current carousel image in a viewport-filling modal above the decision canvas.
3. **Surfaces:** Apply the interaction to repository-rendered card image carousels while preserving carousel navigation, image resizing, deletion controls, and persisted slide selection.

---

## B. Interaction Contract

1. **Pan and zoom library:** Pin `@panzoom/panzoom` to `4.6.2` under `frontend/package.json`; use its local ES module through the existing frontend build path. The selected package is MIT licensed, framework-free, has zero runtime dependencies, supports pointer events and pinch gestures, exposes `minScale` and `maxScale`, and provides `destroy()` cleanup.
2. **Scale range:** Configure the fullscreen image with `minScale: 0.3` and `maxScale: 50`; start at scale `1` and keep panning available throughout the supported range.
3. **Inputs:** Support touch pinch-to-zoom, one-pointer pan, mouse drag, and focal-point mouse-wheel zoom inside the viewer. Prevent these viewer gestures from moving or zooming the underlying canvas.
4. **Close control:** Keep a floating close button fixed at the modal's top-right corner and outside the transformed image surface so it remains reachable at every pan and zoom value.
5. **Lifecycle:** Closing through the fixed button or `Escape` must destroy the Panzoom instance, remove viewer listeners, restore focus to the fullscreen trigger, and leave the carousel on the same slide.
6. **Accessibility:** Expose the viewer as a labelled modal dialog, give both icon buttons accessible names, move focus into the modal on open, trap keyboard focus until close, and lock background scrolling while open.

---

## C. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Reveal:** A tap on the current carousel image reveals the fullscreen control with a visible fade-in without changing slides or opening the viewer directly.
2. **Viewer:** The fullscreen control opens the exact active image, and the fixed close control remains visible while the image is panned and zoomed.
3. **Bounds:** Pinch and wheel input cannot scale below `0.3` or above `50`.
4. **Isolation:** Viewer gestures do not pan or zoom the decision canvas and do not change the saved carousel slide.
5. **Regression:** Automated tests cover active-image selection, affordance reveal, modal open/close, exact scale configuration, focus restoration, listener cleanup, and carousel-state preservation.
6. **Target verification:** On the served `http://127.0.0.1:50150/specs` surface, representative touch input demonstrates the reveal, open, pan, pinch zoom, fixed close control, and unchanged carousel slide after close.
