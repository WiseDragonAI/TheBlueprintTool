## A. Scope

1. **Production mobile swipe:** Use vendored `Embla Carousel 8.6.0` physics on `frontend-mobile`; do not run the custom pointer gesture driver on that surface.
2. **Vertical scrolling:** Mobile users can scroll the page vertically when touching the carousel.
3. **Side navigation:** Render visible previous and next controls over the left and right sides of the image.
4. **Carousel chrome:** Present the filename caption and direct image selectors as compact, legible overlay controls inside the carousel.
5. **Transient title:** When Embla settles on an image, show that image title for `1,000ms`, then fade it to transparent over `260ms` so the full image remains unobstructed.

1. **Gesture limit:** Configure Embla with `dragFree: false`, `slidesToScroll: 1`, and `skipSnaps: false` so a swipe settles on one snap without skipping images.

3. **Controls:** On the served `frontend-mobile` surface, side arrows remain vertically centered over the image; the filename and direct selectors remain inside the carousel frame as compact rounded overlays.
4. **Automated verification:** The focused shared carousel test, frontend TypeScript check, mobile tests, and live `http://127.0.0.1:50150` asset responses pass.
5. **Device gate:** On mobile Brave at `http://127.0.0.1:50150/specs/zone/zone-033dcbfc-f5cf-4d54-ba91-7312bed05e2f/card/card-a1397b46-7583-4bac-b74c-cc758c3a7903`, swipe to the next image and confirm smooth one-slide settling, title appearance after arrival, a `1s` hold, and fade-out.
