## A. Gate Objective

1. Run the complete test and typecheck boundary again after focused groups pass.
2. Re-run code-quality, over-engineering, and bloat analysis on the final diff.

---

## B. Required Evidence

1. Prove preserved behavior, migration correctness, restart semantics, durable data meaning, failure containment, cancellation, recovery, and operator-facing acceptance paths within the declared scope.
2. Tie each final quality finding to changed files and symbols. Fix every in-scope defect as one reviewed cleanup batch, then re-run only the checks invalidated by that batch.
3. Keep deployment and production proof distinct from local verification.

---

## C. Exit Condition

1. The full admitted verification boundary passes and the final diff contains no unresolved in-scope quality finding.
2. End with `READY_FOR_COMMIT_AND_LEARNING`.
