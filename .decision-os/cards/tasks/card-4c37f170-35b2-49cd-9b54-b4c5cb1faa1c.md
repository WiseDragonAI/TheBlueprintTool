## A. Implemented change

1. **Wake-up ownership:** The server-wide scheduler now records every scan request, including requests received while another global scan promise is active.
2. **Catalog synchronization:** Each scan yields once before selecting work, allowing the synchronous startup catalog pass to register every project context.
3. **Drain loop:** The scheduler repeats selection when a wake-up arrives during dispatch, then performs a guarded follow-up scan when a request lands between the last selection and promise cleanup.
4. **Preserved behavior:** FIFO comparison, server-wide capacity, project-local dispatch, interrupted-run handling, and persistence remain unchanged.
