## A. Scope

1. **Objective** `projects` paused and resolved incident occurrences from the rolling `24-hour` window into project-owned failure totals.
2. **Grouping key** `uses` verified project ownership plus `code`, so messages from the same error type remain one group.

---

## B. Completion

1. **Projection** `excludes` occurrences older than `24 hours`, `retains` System-owned evidence, and `preserves` severity and interruption state.
2. **Automated checks** `cover` boundary timestamps, resolved incidents, ownership, equal codes with different messages, and truncated histories.
