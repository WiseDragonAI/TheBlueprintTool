#task-complete

## A. Scope

1. **Implement:** Extend Codex JSONL normalization for `item.type: "todo_list"`.
2. **Preserve:** Stable `item.id`, lifecycle `status`, ordered item text, and boolean completion state.

---

## B. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Started:** An `item.started` todo list normalizes as a durable dedicated event.
2. **Updated:** An `item.updated` snapshot preserves exact order and completion state.
3. **Completed:** An `item.completed` snapshot exposes terminal status without losing checklist content.
