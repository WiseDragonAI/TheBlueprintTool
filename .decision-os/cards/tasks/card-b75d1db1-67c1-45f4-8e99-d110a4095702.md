## A. Root cause and fix

1. `normalizedRunDiagnostics` previously split actionable stderr at every newline and normalized each line as an independent event.
2. The parser now groups continuation lines under a structured timestamped severity header.
3. Classification runs on the complete record, so a multiline `ERROR` remains one error block instead of one error followed by many diagnostics.
