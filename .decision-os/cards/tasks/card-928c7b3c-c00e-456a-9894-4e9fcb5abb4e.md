## C. Sequential Gates

1. **Gate 1 — authored foundations:** G01 and G02 must complete before G03 and G04. Completion means strict store-v2 migration and serialized writers are proven, content records compile, prompt storage/catalog identity is stable, bounded Git operations replace synchronous authored Git, and focused exact-byte commit/recovery/history behavior passes G02 failure injection.
2. **Gate 2 — content consumers:** G03 and G04 may run concurrently after Gate 1. Both must complete before G05 authoring-owner integration is accepted and before canary content proof begins.
3. **Gate 3 — stable editor:** G05 must prove one persistent editable view, separate preview lifetime, read-only navigation, red/blue accessible diffs, and exact modal geometry before G06 replaces card editing and route behavior.
4. **Gate 4 — owner-routed Markdown:** G06 must complete the exact resolver-to-redirect-to-owner-save-to-history chain before delivery admission is allowed to consume direct-path evidence.
5. **Gate 5 — delivery durability:** G07 starts after G02 establishes the shared repository lock. Its valid atomic journal, node receipt, and renewable lease are required before any delivery Git, node, relay, and process mutation.
6. **Gate 6 — node release protocol:** G08 starts after G07. Completion requires isolated reviewed-main promotion support, node-local fetch and reachability proof, immutable release preparation, fixture-proven bootstrap adapters, transport-capability-authenticated fixed node commands, and normal/emergency release identity. It does not mutate a live supervisor.
7. **Gate 7 — relay and admission:** G09 starts only after G06 and G08. Completion requires source-defined dev/production relay separation, frozen project-owning topology, exact-SHA admission, deterministic relay upload/deploy/rollback, and release identity tests.
8. **Gate 8 — delivery state machine:** G10 starts after G09. Completion requires promote, resume, rollback, compensation, stable JSON/exit codes, and phase-boundary fault injection through the single CLI.
9. **Gate 9 — documentation:** G11 starts after G06 and G10 so it records implemented owner and delivery contracts rather than provisional behavior.
10. **Gate 10 — served proof:** G12 starts after G03, G04, G05, G06, G09, G10, and G11. Browser proof must exercise the served canary; only then may scoped Task progress record the evidence.
11. **Gate 11 — candidate verification:** G13/T53 starts after G12. The candidate must be fully verified, committed with required bodies, pushed, clean, equal to `origin/dev`, contain current `origin/main`, and have a complete evidence bundle. It does not create a delivery run.
12. **Gate 12 — production action:** G13/T54 starts only after T53, the fixed CLI/identity/fault tests, and the operator-provided phone supervisor record. Bootstrap every active project-owning node through its validated local profile, then invoke the explicit promotion; `promote` creates the journal and accepts success only for durable `complete`.
