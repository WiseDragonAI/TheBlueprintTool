## C. Formatting failure RCA

1. I treated each operator correction as an isolated defect instead of revalidating the complete `master-task` summary and `thread reply` against every injected instruction.
2. I conflated the two artifacts: the `master-task` requires complete strategic state, while the `thread reply` permits only newly material outcomes.
3. I interpreted the backtick rule as a restriction rather than consistently applying it to supporting mechanisms and states such as `lys`, `Exec`, `failed`, `task-execution`, and `project-sync`.
4. I checked raw Markdown presence instead of the rendered result, allowing sparse formatting and a nested `Exec` reference to render incorrectly.
5. I added UUIDs, verification inventory, and unchanged state because I optimized for evidentiary completeness rather than **CTO decision value**.

## D. Strategic constraints

1. Durable history in `project-sync/runs.json` **must not masquerade as active execution**.
2. Recovery must preserve one owner for each `originFingerprint` and prevent duplicate `project-sync` attempts.
3. The `master-task` must contain context, objective, verified state, constraints, decision, blocker, credible path, expected outcome, and open questions.
4. The `thread reply` must contain only material iteration outcomes, without UUIDs, routine verification, process narration, or unchanged task state.
5. Backticks identify exact mechanisms, states, and dependencies; bold identifies **strategic importance inside sentences**.

3. Rendered output, not raw Markdown tokens, is the final formatting authority.
4. This direction advances the objective by correcting both the misleading `failed` to `task-execution` lifecycle and the repeated summary-format drift.
