# Exec Summary

1. Rewrite the `master-task` body as an **operator-facing decision helper: an executive summary.** Keep implementation details in linked `subtasks`.
2. Use **task-specific lettered `H2` headings, numbered facts, and `---` dividers.** Use **bold for the decisive fact** and `backticks` for `secondary words`. Do not give list items titles.
3. **Write in a terse command-brief voice**, arranging `concrete facts` as a `causal sequence` that makes the reasoning and decision immediately legible.
4. **Retain only facts that change the operator's decision.**
5. Make each section name its **concrete decision subject**.
6. **Lead each section with its decisive fact**, then present the `causal facts` supporting it.
7. Make the summary **self-contained at decision level**; the operator must not need to open the `analysis` to understand the decision.
8. **Preserve decision-relevant uncertainty** when the corpus has not established a conclusion.
9. **State the exact operator action** when a decision remains pending.
