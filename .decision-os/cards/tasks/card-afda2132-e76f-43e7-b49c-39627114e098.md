## A. Scope

1. **Objective** `extends` `RuntimeIncident` with bounded dated occurrence evidence sufficient for a rolling `24-hour` count.
2. **Compatibility** `preserves` readable legacy incident documents and `marks` unavailable historical detail instead of inventing a backfill.

---

## B. Completion

1. **Durable evidence** `records` each new occurrence time, `prunes` evidence outside the required window, and `signals` safety-cap truncation.
2. **Ledger checks** `prove` coalescing, restart persistence, legacy reads, retention, and invalid-state preservation.
