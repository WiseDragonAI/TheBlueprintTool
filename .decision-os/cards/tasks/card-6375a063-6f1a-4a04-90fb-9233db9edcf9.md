## A. Purpose

1. **Master-task objective:** Build mutual clarity in an important relationship by replacing assumptions with explicit statements about intentions, expectations, uncertainties, inner experience, and the meaning of the relationship.
2. **Human workflow:** Each person first clarifies their own position, prepares direct questions, holds one structured conversation, then records only the points both people genuinely understand.

---

## B. Product Reality

1. **Current capability:** Decision OS provides a card-owned `LLM → operator` questionnaire with choices, custom text, voice capture, deferral, progress, and durable card mutation.
2. **Verified boundary:** The current data model stores responses by question ID only. It has no second-participant identity, answer attribution, shared visibility, comparison, consent, agreement acknowledgement, or mutual-completion state.
3. **Acceptance gap:** No implemented signal proves that both people reached shared understanding, acknowledged differences, or accepted the recorded result.

---

## C. Current Decision

1. **Proceed with individual clarification first:** define personal intentions, expectations, uncertainties, desired communication depth, and explicit questions before the mutual conversation.
2. **Do not treat questionnaire completion as mutual clarity:** the existing single-operator flow can prepare one participant, but it cannot represent the shared outcome.

---

## D. Blocking Gap

1. **Specification gap:** Participant identity, privacy, consent, bilateral review, answer comparison, mutual acknowledgement, completion criteria, and recovery behavior remain undefined.
2. **Task-graph gap:** Generated pipeline cards remain attached as subtasks. The intended actionable relationship-task structure has not been established, so the master task remains `todo`.
