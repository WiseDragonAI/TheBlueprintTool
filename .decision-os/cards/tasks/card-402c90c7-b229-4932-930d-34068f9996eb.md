## A. Regression Boundary

1. Extend focused federation tests with two connected replicas and a relay transport that deliberately drops the terminal execution batch.
2. Keep the connection and catalog stable so reconnect hooks cannot repair the state.
3. Trigger the periodic reconciliation cycle and prove the remote execution advances from revision `4/running` to revision `5/succeeded`.
4. Assert equal roots, zero missing buckets, zero dirty entities, and zero pending deliveries after acknowledgement.

---

## B. Failure Cases

1. Prove repeated cycles are idempotent.
2. Prove one project reconciliation failure does not block another project.
