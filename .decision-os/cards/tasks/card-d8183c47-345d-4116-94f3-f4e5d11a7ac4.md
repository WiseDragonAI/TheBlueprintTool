## A. Incident Evidence

1. **Authoritative workstation state:** execution `codex-execution-1785228468876-b3175a78` reached `succeeded`, lifecycle revision `5`, at `2026-07-28T09:16:40.579Z`.
2. **Stale phone replica:** the same execution remained lifecycle revision `4`, phase `running`, with `finishedAt: null`.
3. **Repair gap:** the phone's relay root last converged at `2026-07-28T09:14:31.405Z`, before the terminal transition, and exposed no pending delivery that would force another repair.
4. **Read-path evidence:** direct workstation status returned the terminal record, but the phone status proxy returned that response without merging revision `5` into its durable replicated task store.

---

## B. Objective

1. Preserve immediate mutation push as the primary low-latency replication path.
2. Activate the existing root, bucket-summary, missing-bucket, and acknowledgement protocol as a bounded periodic anti-entropy loop.
3. Ensure a missed terminal execution delta converges without requiring a node reconnect, catalog change, server restart, or manual operator action.

---

## C. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Fast path preserved:** every task-state mutation continues to call `publishDelta()` immediately.
2. **Periodic repair:** local project roots are advertised to the relay and remote project subscriptions are reconciled on a bounded interval.
3. **Lifecycle ownership:** reconciliation cycles cannot overlap, timers do not keep the process alive, and shutdown clears all scheduler resources.
4. **Failure containment:** a failed reconciliation cycle is recorded within the federation scope and does not stop unrelated projects or the server.
5. **Regression proof:** an automated test drops the pushed terminal lifecycle contribution, keeps both nodes connected, then proves a later periodic cycle changes the replica from revision `4/running` to revision `5/succeeded`.
6. **Convergence proof:** diagnostics show equal roots, no missing buckets, no runtime-dirty entities, and no pending delivery after repair.
7. **Live verification:** the workstation-owned execution becomes terminal on the phone without reloading state through a restart.

---

## D. Scope Boundary

1. Reuse the existing federation state protocol; do not introduce a second synchronization protocol.
2. Durable outbox persistence and status-read repair remain separate hardening work unless the dropped-push regression proves periodic root repair cannot satisfy the acceptance criteria.
3. Preserve invalid durable state byte-for-byte and use public task-state APIs in tests.
