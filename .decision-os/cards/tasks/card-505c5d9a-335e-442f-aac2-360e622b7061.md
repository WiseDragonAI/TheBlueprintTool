Ledger: Specs

## A. Executive outcome

1. Every new Codex card run now receives a **living strategic-summary contract written for a CTO**.
2. The master task is the **single durable view of the complete task state**, not a record of the latest run.
3. The thread reply is a separate `iteration record` containing only **short, material outcomes from that turn**.

---

## B. Strategic reading model

1. The summary must connect the **global context, objective, verified state, constraints, choices, blocker, path, and expected outcome**.
2. It must explain **why the chosen direction advances the objective**, allowing the CTO to judge strategic coherence.
3. Bold emphasizes **decision-critical ideas inside sentences** and never acts as a list-item label.
4. Backticks identify supporting `mechanisms`, `system states`, and `dependencies` only when they clarify the strategic choice.
5. Routine implementation detail stays outside the executive summary unless the **decision cannot be understood without it**.

---

## C. Thread-reply boundary

1. Replies contain a **small number of very short numbered bullets** with no heading or section.
2. They report only a material `result`, `decision`, `resolved blocker`, `new blocker`, or `changed risk` from the current iteration.
3. They exclude `analysis`, `rationale`, `process narration`, full-task summaries, and implicit workflow actions.
4. All reasoning required to understand the overall task remains in the master task.

---

## D. Verification and residual risk

1. The **prompt-builder tests, launch-route tests, and backend typecheck pass** with the new contract.
2. The backend suite exposed an unrelated `federation connector timeout` that reproduces in isolation and does not touch this prompt path.
3. The implementation is merged on `main`; no product decision remains open.
