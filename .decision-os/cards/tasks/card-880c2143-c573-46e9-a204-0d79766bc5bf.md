## A. Implementation

1. **Collapsed dock:** Default the mobile composer to `.is-mobile-text-collapsed`, hide the textarea, and render `FILE`, `TEXT`, and `REC` in equal-width columns.
2. **Voice sizing:** Set all three dock buttons to `66px`, matching the existing voice recorder control height.
3. **Text expansion:** Route `toggle-thread-text` to reveal and focus a `75dvh` textarea.
4. **Successful collapse:** After `submitThreadDraft()` clears a successfully committed draft, restore `.is-mobile-text-collapsed`; retain expanded mode when submission does not clear the draft.
5. **Desktop isolation:** Keep `TEXT` and `SEND` hidden outside the mobile breakpoint and preserve the desktop composer.
