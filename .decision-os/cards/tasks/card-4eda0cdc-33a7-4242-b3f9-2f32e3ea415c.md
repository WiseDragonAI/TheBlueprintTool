## A. Gate Objective

1. Commit the verified intended change set with the repository-required subject and `WHAT` plus `WHY` body.
2. Capture concise lessons about avoidable defects and measurable workflow improvements.

---

## B. Required Evidence

1. Inspect the exact staged subset, preserve unrelated work, verify the complete commit message, push through the approved identity, and distinguish pushed state from deployment state.
2. Explain each avoidable issue through its first missed gate, the evidence that would have caught it earlier, and the reusable guard that now prevents recurrence.
3. Audit run telemetry and session artifacts for repeated searches, redundant verification, sequentializable waits, context loss, and ad hoc tooling. Select one concrete speed improvement backed by observed cost.

---

## C. Exit Condition

1. The intended verified subset is committed and pushed, lessons are actionable, the reusable tooling decision is recorded, and remaining deployment proof is explicit.
2. Keep the master task active until direct operator closeout authorization is received.
