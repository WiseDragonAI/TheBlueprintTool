## A. Feature Direction

1. **Headless session visibility:** The control room shows the active Codex headless sessions and a timer for each session.
2. **New task intake:** Starting a task creates a card in a temporary zone where voice notes can be added. The task may include instructions for classifying the card into a zone and ledger.
3. **Task status visibility:** After a task is launched, the agent updates its status when card processing finishes so the card can surface in the control room. The control room also provides access to cards and tasks that are waiting.

---

## B. Task And Card Relationship

1. **Master task card:** A task may be represented by a specially labeled master card linked to a zone.
2. **Sub-cards:** One task can relate to several cards. Cards created as sub-tasks are linked through the task zone and therefore associated with the master card.
3. **Open direction:** The exact relationship between the master card, its zone, and its sub-cards remains to be clarified.
