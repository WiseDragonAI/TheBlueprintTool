## A. Required Result

1. **Purpose:** write and update the master card as a high-signal executive decision brief for a CTO.
2. **Pipeline placement:** invoke after Pipeline 1 `Codebase analysis` and after Pipeline 2 `Problem and solution explanation`.
3. **Evidence:** consume the original request, linked specs, memories, repository findings, question state, tasks, adversarial architectures, and selected solution without inventing missing facts.
4. **Question output:** preserve one stable questionnaire identity and write revision-bound questions through the native card questionnaire contract.

---

## B. Completion Signals

1. **First screen:** executive summary states the decision, stage, recommendation, and material consequence.
2. **Decision support:** verified evidence, unknowns, tradeoffs, risks, rejected extremes, task coverage, and verification strategy are present and traceable.
3. **Revision safety:** a changed context revision clears prior answers and invalidates the downstream gate.
4. **Continuity:** later invocations preserve operator decisions, delivery evidence, QA feedback, and lessons provenance.
5. **No bloat:** raw logs, repeated narrative, and generic process claims are excluded.
