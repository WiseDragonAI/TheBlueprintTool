1. Run the existing relationship-backed migration to move only misplaced master-task zones and their task graphs into the canonical task authority.
2. Move task cards, zones, relationships, annotations, lifecycle state, and task-owned references with collision and integrity checks.
3. Preserve the complete specification corpus, its stable IDs, relationships, annotations, MasterLedger links, and registry entry.

2. Every pre-migration specification ID and relationship remains intact.
