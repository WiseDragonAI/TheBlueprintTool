## A. Result

1. **Implemented and verified:** the desktop thread inspector no longer casts an outward shadow onto the master-task center.
2. The master task body remains solid, and regular card split views retain their prior inspector styling.

---

## B. Root Cause

1. `.mobile-thread-inspector` owned `box-shadow: -18px 0 42px rgba(0,0,0,.34)` at desktop widths.
2. Its negative horizontal offset projected the blurred shadow left of the fixed thread panel into the master-task surface.
3. The prior correction inspected only `.ledger-card-body`, so it missed this overlapping sibling layer.

---

## C. Correction

1. `renderCard` sets `data-master-task` on `#card-view` from the parsed card contract.
2. When the thread is open and that value is `true`, `.mobile-thread-inspector` uses `box-shadow: none`.
3. The thread panel's inset shadow, border, and regular-card inspector shadow are unchanged.

---

## D. Verification

1. The focused regression, frontend typecheck, and full frontend suite pass.
2. A fresh served master-task split view returned `200` and computed a solid task body plus `box-shadow: none` on the inspector.
3. A fresh served regular-card split view returned `200` and retained the existing inspector shadow, confirming the scope boundary.
