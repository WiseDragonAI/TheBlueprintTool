## A. Objective

1. Make recovery, adopted monitoring, cancellation, and dispatch failure obey exact execution ownership.

---

## B. Verified Evidence

1. Recovery compares only run identity, cleanup omits execution identity, and pending recovery lacks runtime.
2. Adopted monitoring trusts terminal JSONL before PID exit.
3. Adopted cancellation omits settlement; dispatch failure drops the item and stops advancement.

---

## C. Implementation Contract

1. Require equality across card, queue, runtime, and payload execution identities for every transition.
2. Rebuild pending runtime state on startup.
3. Use one process-tree termination helper and settle after exit plus stream closure.
4. Dispatch failure writes failure, clears matching ownership, publishes, and continues scheduling.
5. Recovery retains the exact card lease and discards older contradicted execution.

---

## D. Acceptance Tests

1. Older execution cannot recover after newer continuation.
2. Pending work is cancellable after restart.
3. Terminal JSONL cannot settle a surviving process.
4. Adopted cancel permits immediate deletion.
5. Dispatch failure advances the next item.
