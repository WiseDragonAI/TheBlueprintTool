## A. Required Result

1. **QA stage:** after Pipeline 3 `Report`, wait for explicit operator acceptance or rejection.
2. **Accept:** bind acceptance to the tested implementation revision and authorize Pipeline 4.
3. **Reject:** persist QA feedback and restart Pipeline 1 `Context Analysis`, including complete codebase reassessment before new questions.

---

## B. Completion Signals

1. **No false completion:** entering or passing QA does not mark the master task done.
2. **Durability:** QA evidence and outcome survive refresh and server restart.
3. **Restart evidence:** stale questions, answers, solution acceptance, and generated artifacts cannot be mistaken for the new attempt.
