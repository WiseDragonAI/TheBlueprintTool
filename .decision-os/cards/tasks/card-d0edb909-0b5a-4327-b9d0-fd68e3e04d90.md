## A. Scope

1. Implement the sole local issuer, append-only segmented event store, identity and emission-bucket indexes, durable per-peer pending sets, and atomic materialized projection.
2. Implement versioned state snapshots containing reducer version, projection checksum, conflict state, covered bucket manifests, size, and content checksum.
3. Expose snapshot manifest export, verified snapshot installation, fallback selection, and projection rebuild for fresh and recovered nodes.
4. Bound normal replay by loading the newest compatible verified snapshot and applying only its uncovered event tail.
5. Detect a late change inside covered history, select the newest preceding valid snapshot, replay forward, invalidate later checkpoints, and regenerate them.
6. Seal event segments for reconciliation and archival without deleting audit history.

---

## B. Acceptance

1. Crash tests at append, segment seal, snapshot write, manifest commit, snapshot install, index update, and projection replacement recover every accepted event.
2. A blank node installs a transferred snapshot only after schema, manifest, size, and checksum verification, then reconstructs from its missing tail.
3. A year-scale fixture restores from the newest snapshot plus a bounded tail rather than replaying genesis.
4. Late insertion before the newest checkpoint matches clean replay and replaces every invalidated later snapshot.
5. Corrupt and incompatible snapshots fall back to the preceding valid checkpoint.
6. Deleting the compatibility export and replay indexes reconstructs them from the snapshot chain and event tail.
