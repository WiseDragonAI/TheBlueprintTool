## A. Scope

1. **Objective:** extract the voice widget design into shared mobile tokens and reusable control variants in `frontend-mobile/assets/mobile.css`.
2. **Reference:** preserve the `3px` geometry, dark gradients, inset edges, internal highlight, accent glow, and pressed depth from `frontend/assets/canvas/terminal-button.css`.

---

## B. Requirements

1. **Tokens:** define semantic colors, borders, shadows, radius, spacing, `44px` target size, easing, and `120ms`, `180ms`, and `240ms` durations.
2. **Primitives:** cover icon, primary, secondary, destructive, tab, chip, row, and recessed-input controls.
3. **States:** provide shared `rest`, `hover`, `focus-visible`, `active`, `selected`, `disabled`, and `loading` treatments.
4. **Typography:** retain Ubuntu and replace component-local control typography with shared label tokens.

---

## C. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Reuse:** migrated controls consume shared tokens and primitives instead of duplicating the visual contract.
2. **Semantics:** selection uses `aria-selected="true"` and `aria-pressed="true"`; loading preserves dimensions and prevents duplicate activation.

---

## D. Implementation

1. **Complete:** shared tokens and primitives are implemented in `frontend-mobile/assets/mobile.css`, including `3px` geometry, inset depth, accent glow, `44px` targets, semantic states, and the `120ms`, `180ms`, and `240ms` motion scale.
