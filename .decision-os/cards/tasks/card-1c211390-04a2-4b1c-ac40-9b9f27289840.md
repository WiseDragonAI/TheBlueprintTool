#master-task

Ledger: Specs

## A. Scope

1. **Application drawer:** Expose `Control room`, `Ledgers`, `Pipelines`, and `Skill library` as the four global destinations.
2. **Header cleanup:** Remove the top-bar pipeline shortcut and the duplicated control-room title and `Live` indicator.
3. **Task creation:** Place `New task` fourth in the fixed bottom control-room action bar.

---

## C. Acceptance Criteria

1. **Drawer:** No individual workspace ledger appears directly in the drawer.
2. **Ledgers:** The `Ledgers` destination opens the complete ledger list.
3. **Libraries:** `Pipelines` and `Skill library` open their respective libraries.
4. **Header:** The top bar has no `P` button, and the control room does not repeat its title or display `Live`.
5. **Bottom bar:** `New task` is the fourth control-room action and creates the existing task intake.
