---
name: exec-summary
description: Rewrite Decision OS master-task bodies as efficient, factual, operator-facing executive summaries. Use when a master task must make its reasoning, decision, uncertainty, and pending operator action logical without changing linked subtasks.
---

1. `Rewrite` the `master-task` **body** as an operator-facing **decision helper**: an **executive summary**. Do not `change` the **subtasks**.
2. Use task-specific lettered `H2` **headings**, numbered **facts**, and `---` **dividers**. Treat inline **emphasis** as semantic syntax: bold only decision-bearing **nouns** or **noun phrases**; wrap the operative or causal `verb` connecting them, and every exact `literal`, in backticks; leave grammar plain. Never bold a complete clause or sentence. Do not give list items **titles**.
3. `Use` efficient, factual, **military tone**. `Arrange` concrete **facts** as a **causal sequence** that `makes` the **reasoning** and **decision** logical.
4. Retain only **facts** that `change` the operator's **decision**.
5. Make each **section** `name` its **decision subject**.
6. `Lead` each **section** with its decisive **fact**, then `present` the causal **facts** supporting it.
7. `Preserve` **decision-relevant uncertainty** when the **corpus** has not `established` a **conclusion**.
8. `State` the exact **operator action** when a **decision** remains `pending`.
